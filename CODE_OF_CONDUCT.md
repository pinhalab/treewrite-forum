# Code of Conduct

The TreeWrite Forum is a place to ask questions, report problems, share ideas and show what you've built with TreeWrite. To keep it welcoming and useful for everyone, all participants are expected to follow this Code of Conduct.

## Our pledge

We want this forum to be a friendly and respectful space for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and kind, even when you disagree
- Assume good intent and give others the benefit of the doubt
- Give constructive feedback, focused on ideas and not on people
- Stay on topic and post in the right category
- Search before posting to avoid duplicate discussions
- When reporting a problem, include enough details for others to reproduce it (TreeWrite version, operating system, steps to reproduce)
- In Q&A, mark the answer that solved your question so others can find it later

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory, offensive, or sexually explicit content
- Trolling or deliberately provocative comments
- Spam, advertising, or unrelated self promotion
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Sharing malicious links or files
- Pressuring maintainers for responses, features, or release dates

## Moderation

Maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may edit, hide, lock, or delete any content, and temporarily or permanently block participants whose behavior goes against these rules.

Depending on severity, actions usually follow this order:

1. **Reminder**: a note about the expected behavior
2. **Warning**: the content is hidden or removed, with a warning
3. **Temporary block**: the participant cannot interact for a period of time
4. **Permanent block**: the participant is removed from the forum

Severe violations may lead directly to a permanent block.

## Reporting

If you see something that breaks this Code of Conduct, use the **Report content** option in the `...` menu of the discussion or comment, or contact the maintainers at [support@pinhalab.com](mailto:support@pinhalab.com). All reports are handled confidentially.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces of the TreeWrite Forum, including discussions, comments, and polls, as well as interactions between members that originate from it.
